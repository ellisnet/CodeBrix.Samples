This file documents third-party content bundled with, or used at run time by,
the JustBetweenUs sample application, together with the licenses under which
that content is provided.

Third-party CODE dependencies are consumed as NuGet packages. Each package
carries its own license and third-party notices in its own repository/package

The JustBetweenUs application is based on, and was inspired by, a code sample 
provided by Paul Ainsworth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Android head  (CodeBrixPlatform/JustBetweenUs.Android)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Android head contains no third-party files of its own. Its build brings
in, as NuGet packages, the following third-party code that ends up inside the
Android application package:

  - Material Components for Android and the AndroidX libraries (Google), in
    their .NET bindings (Xamarin.Google.Android.Material, Xamarin.AndroidX.*),
    licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. They arrive through the
    CodeBrix.Android package, which draws every control with them.

  - The Android native builds of SkiaSharp and HarfBuzzSharp
    (SkiaSharp.NativeAssets.Android, HarfBuzzSharp.NativeAssets.Android),
    licensed under the MIT License, with the Skia (BSD 3-Clause) and HarfBuzz
    (Old MIT) libraries they bundle. They arrive through
    CodeBrix.Android.SkiaSharp.Views and CodeBrix.Android.Svg, and draw the
    SVG icons and the Lottie star on the device.

Each of those packages carries its own license and notices, and the
CodeBrix.Android packages ship their own THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt; they are
not reproduced here.
