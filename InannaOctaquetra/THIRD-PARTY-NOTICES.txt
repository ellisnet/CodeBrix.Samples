THIRD-PARTY NOTICES
===================

This file documents third-party content that is bundled with, or used at run
time by, the InannaOctaquetra sample application, together with the licenses under
which that content is provided.

Third-party CODE dependencies are consumed as NuGet packages. Each package
carries its own license and third-party notices in its own repository/package
(the CodeBrix.* packages ship their own THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt), so those are
not reproduced here. That includes the PDF document library the report is drawn
with.

Android head  (src/InannaOctaquetra.Android)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Android head contains no third-party files of its own. Its build brings
in, as NuGet packages, the following third-party code that ends up inside the
Android application package:

  - Material Components for Android and the AndroidX libraries (Google), in
    their .NET bindings (Xamarin.Google.Android.Material, Xamarin.AndroidX.*),
    licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. They arrive through the
    CodeBrix.Android package, which draws every control with them.

The embedded Merriweather faces described above travel inside the
InannaOctaquetra.Reading assembly on Android exactly as on the desktop heads, and
the CodeBrix.Platform.Fonts.Merriweather copy reaches the Android package as
application assets. Each of those packages carries its own license and
notices, and the CodeBrix.Android packages ship their own
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt; they are not reproduced here.

The inscription on the Octaquetra: ETCSL text and the Oracc sign list
  (Packaging/Artwork/Tablet/tablet-E.json, build_tablet.py, SOURCES.md;
   rendered into src/libs/InannaOctaquetra.Reading/Art/octaquetra-inscribed.png)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The signs engraved along the gold band are Inana's claiming of the ME from the
Sumerian composition "Inana and Enki", Segment E, lines 1-36, as edited by The
Electronic Text Corpus of Sumerian Literature (ETCSL), composition c.1.3.1:
  https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/cgi-bin/etcsl.cgi?text=c.1.3.1
The ancient text itself is in the public domain. The ETCSL transliteration of
those 36 lines is reproduced line by line in tablet-E.json. The ETCSL edition
is "(c) Copyright 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006 The ETCSL project, Faculty of Oriental
Studies, University of Oxford"; the ETCSL site publishes no open license and
asks that requests for permission to cite be addressed to
orient(AT)orinst.ox.ac.uk.

Each reading was mapped to its Unicode cuneiform character through the Oracc
Global Sign List (https://github.com/oracc/osl, 00lib/osl.asl). The sign list
itself is not included here: build_tablet.py reads a local copy of it, and only
the resulting sign names and Unicode code points appear in tablet-E.json. The
osl repository does not state a license.

The Octaquetra of Inanna artwork (Packaging/Artwork/Octaquetra-of-inanna.svg,
carried in EmblemArt.cs as path data), the royal purple velvet pictures
(src/libs/InannaOctaquetra.Reading/Art/velvet.jpg and velvet-print.jpg), the

(MIT license); the tool is not part of the application or its solution.

Card texts, emblem art and interpretation templates
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The forty cards' names, epithets, transliterations, domains, lore, keywords,
meanings and invocations, the nine stations of the Octaquetra spread, the
procedural vector art for every emblem and ornament, and the sentence stock the
interpreter writes from are all original to this application. They draw on
Mesopotamian mythology, which is in the public domain; apart from the
inscription credited above, no third-party text, translation or artwork is
reproduced or redistributed here.

Readings and reports (written at run time)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application writes reading files and PDF reports where the user chooses.
That content belongs to whoever made it; nothing of it is redistributed as part
of this repository, and nothing is downloaded at run time.
