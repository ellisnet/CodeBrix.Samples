FFmpeg (used at run time, not bundled)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When a selected Notion page contains video or audio, the application invokes
the ffmpeg and ffprobe executables already installed on the host system (via
the CodeBrix.VideoProcessing package) to extract a poster frame and probe the
media duration. FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org) is licensed under the LGPL 2.1 or
later, with optional components under the GPL; consult the license of the
ffmpeg build installed on your system. No FFmpeg binaries are bundled with, or
distributed by, this application, and the application degrades gracefully
(rendering a media card instead of a poster frame) when ffmpeg is absent.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Android head  (src/NotionDocumentCreator.Android)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Android head contains no third-party files of its own. Its build brings
in, as NuGet packages, the following third-party code that ends up inside the
Android application package:

  - Material Components for Android and the AndroidX libraries (Google), in
    their .NET bindings (Xamarin.Google.Android.Material, Xamarin.AndroidX.*),
    licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. They arrive through the
    CodeBrix.Android package, which draws every control with them.

No FFmpeg binaries reach the Android application package either: the
CodeBrix.VideoProcessing package is managed code only, and on Android, where
no ffmpeg exists, video and audio render as media cards.

Each of those packages carries its own license and notices, and the
CodeBrix.Android packages ship their own THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt; they are
not reproduced here.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Notion page content and media (downloaded at run time)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This sample fetches page content and media files from the user's own Notion
workspace (https://www.notion.so) through the Notion API, using the user's own
integration token, and composes them into a PDF. The rights to that content
remain with its owners; the person running the application is responsible for
having the right to reproduce the content they select.
